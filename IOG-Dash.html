        Calendar rules

        Four rules that keep several launches from landing on the same audience.

        One calendar

        Every venture announcement goes on a shared calendar before any asset is briefed.

        Five working days apart

        No two venture announcements within five working days on channels they share.

        Holiday quiet period

        No launches from December 21 to January 2.

        Same gates every time

        Claims, regions and chain checks run before any launch, whichever venture it is.

        Growth that holds

        The community rung is made of four signals. Each counts people who did something, not people who showed up once. Together they replace the follower count as the number marketing leads with.

        Contribute

        Weekly active contributors

        Members who posted, replied, voted, filed an issue or shipped something this week.

        Help

        Member-answered share

        Questions answered by another member, not staff, within 24 hours.

        Trust

        Support around decisions

        Sentiment before a vote or a release, then the same measure afterward.

        Stay

        Week-8 retention

        Share of each month's new members still active eight weeks later, tracked by cohort.

          Community that grows without hype

          The ladder ends in community health, and the funnel ends in contributors. This view shows how marketing would run that layer week to week, and how it would prepare the one community moment with a public deadline: the governance vote.

          Principle 1

          Count people who did something

          A member counts when they post, reply, vote or ship. Joining does not count.

          Principle 2

          Reward contribution, not attention

          Pay for answers, tutorials and bug reports. Do not pay for follows.

          Principle 3

          Say less and prove more

          Talk about what shipped. Use reviewed wording wherever a claim touches money.

          Principle 4

          Make decisions legible early

          Explain each proposal in plain language weeks before voting opens.

          One coordinator, many teams

          Marketing for four ventures and a chain will run through agencies, freelancers and contractors. This view shows how one coordinator keeps outside teams moving without turning internal review into the bottleneck, however large or small the internal team is.

          Rule 1

          One intake, one calendar

          Every request enters one queue and lands on the shared launch calendar. No side channels.

          Rule 2

          One accountable owner per item

          Many people contribute. Exactly one person is accountable for the decision.

          Rule 3

          Review is the scarce resource

          Standard briefs and pre-approved wording cut the hours that internal reviewers spend per item.

          Rule 4

          Outside teams report reach, never alone

          Reach always appears next to the rung above it on the KPI ladder.

          Telling the AI story honestly

          IO has several AI efforts in public. The voice rule for all of them is the same: show it working, say what it is, and name what it is not yet.

          Public itemEvidenceSourceVoice rule
          Midnight City: a live simulation where autonomous AI agents live, work and transact. Public since Feb 26, 2026. Version 2 launched Jul 13, 2026.PublicMidnight blog, Feb and Jul 2026Show the agents deciding something, label it a demo, and say what it proves about privacy.
          An AI and blockchain lab in Athens was announced.PublicIO release, Apr 20, 2026Say what it will research and when results will arrive. Make no capability claims until work ships.
          Integra and Overmind are listed as internal AI tools.PublicIO's own product listing, as found Nov 5, 2026Treat them as internal until IO says otherwise, and ask what can be shown.
          Rule 1

          Show, do not claim

          A working demo persuades more than a capability list.

          Rule 2

          Label demo and product

          Readers should always know which one they are looking at.

          Rule 3

          Name the limits

          Say what it cannot do yet. That is what makes the rest believable.
