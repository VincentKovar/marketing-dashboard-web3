The first 90 days

Each phase ends with something the executive team can see.

Days 1 to 30

Audit and listen

  • Inventory every partner, contract and monthly cost
  • Read the brand voice across each product and venture
  • Baseline the funnel and community numbers
  • One-to-ones with executives, sales and finance
Days 31 to 60

Install the system

  • Partner scorecards and a standard brief template
  • Attribution rules agreed with finance in writing
  • Voice guide version one with dials per venture
  • Weekly rhythm running with the coordinator
Days 61 to 90

Ship and prove

  • First integrated launch, planned to the launch workflow
  • First commercial contribution report to the executive team
  • Community retention programme live on the top two channels
  • Roster decisions: keep, fix or replace

Operating rhythm

Reporting cadence that keeps marketing tied to sales and finance targets.

Weekly, Friday

Flash report

One page: spend, pipeline, community health, partner status. The coordinator assembles it and the Head adds the so-what.

Monthly

Commercial review

With sales and finance: attribution model, CAC, payback, retention. Decisions on moving spend between channels.

Quarterly

Executive readout

Plan versus result, roster changes, next quarter bets. The internal narrative is refreshed so staff and the public hear the same story.
